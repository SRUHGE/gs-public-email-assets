Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Guided Startup respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you sign up for early access to our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for early access, we may collect:

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Your business interests, if provided
  • Your device type and operating system
  • Your consent to receive communications from Guided Startup

We may also maintain records of when you registered and when communications were sent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Confirm your early-access registration
  • Send updates about Guided Startup's development and launch
  • Invite selected subscribers to participate in testing
  • Improve our services and early-access experience
  • Respond to questions and support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Store and Protect Information

We use service providers to store registration information and deliver emails.

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that help operate Guided Startup, including database hosting and email delivery providers.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

5. Email Communications

By registering for early access and agreeing to receive communications, you authorize Guided Startup to send you updates about its development, testing opportunities, and launch.

You may unsubscribe from optional email communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting:

support@guidedstartup.com

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Cookies and Technical Information

Our website may use necessary browser storage and technical information to operate its features.

If we introduce additional analytics, advertising technologies, or optional tracking, we will update this policy as appropriate.

8. Children's Privacy

Guided Startup's early-access program is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Guided Startup develops.

When changes are made, we will update the effective date shown above.
